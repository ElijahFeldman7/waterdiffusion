Those little sores you get on your tongue from eating too much salt or sugar are called "cheilosis" or "tongue ulcers." They are usually caused by a combination of factors, including:

1. Irritation from spicy foods or drinks
2. Poor oral hygiene
3. Nutritional deficiencies
4. Stress or anxiety
5. Certain medical conditions

To get rid of tongue sores, you can try the following:

1. **Avoid irritants**: Try to avoid spicy foods, drinks, and flavors that can irritate your tongue.
2. **Practice good oral hygiene**: Brush your teeth and tongue gently with a soft-bristled toothbrush and mild toothpaste.
3. **Stay hydrated**: Drink plenty of water to keep your mouth and tongue moist.
4. **Use a soothing mouthwash**: Try using a gentle mouthwash that contains chamomile or other calming ingredients to help soothe the sores.
5. **Eat a balanced diet**: Ensure you're getting enough vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients to support your overall health.
6. **Manage stress**: Try to reduce stress and anxiety through relaxation techniques like meditation, yoga, or deep breathing exercises.
7. **Topical treatments**: Your doctor may recommend using topical creams or ointments to help heal the sores.

If your tongue sores are severe or persistent, it's best to consult a healthcare professional to rule out any underlying medical conditions.
